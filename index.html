Number Sums

How to play

Goal

Each puzzle shows numbers in a grid with colored regions. Choose which numbers count toward the puzzle by circling them. The numbers you circle in each row, column, and region must add up to that line’s target.

Targets

  • Rows — totals on the left
  • Columns — totals on top
  • Regions — totals on the small badge in each colored area

A target turns green when your circled numbers in that row, column, or region add up correctly. You can still be wrong overall, so check every line before you finish.

Circle and Erase

  • Circle — tap a cell to mark its number as included (circled). Tap again to clear the mark.
  • Erase — tap to mark a number as excluded (crossed out). Tap again to clear.

Pick the mode with the buttons below the board. Keyboard shortcuts: C for Circle, E for Erase.

Controls

  • New Game — random puzzle at the selected difficulty
  • Reset — clear your marks on the current puzzle
  • Undo — reverse your last mark (or ⌘/Ctrl+Z)
  • Difficulty — Easy through Expert; larger grids are harder

Winning

You solve the puzzle when every cell is marked included or excluded, and every row, column, and region sum matches its target.

Puzzle complete!

Circle included numbers so each row, column, and region matches its target.
